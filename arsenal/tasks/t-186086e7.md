Rearrange the one slim bar (D-19, status/ideas.md I-25, I-29), as in Chrome's PDF viewer: left ☰ outline, title, Back/Forward; centre zoom − % +; right Find, Print and a ⋮ More button. The File/View/Go/Help menus fold into ⋮ More as one menu with their sections; every command keeps its shortcut. Bar order lives in `src/ui/definition.ts` (`bar`, `menus`). Write `test_bar_has_three_zones_and_more_menu` in `src/ui/shell.test.ts` first: the bar's left/centre/right groups hold those items in that order, no text menus are in the bar, and ⋮ More opens a menu listing every former menu item.
