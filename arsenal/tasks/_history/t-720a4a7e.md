Ctrl+Tab activates the next tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab the previous one, wrapping at the ends (D-22, status/ideas.md I-28); list them in the shortcuts sheet (F1). Shortcuts are in `src/ui/definition.ts`. Write `test_ctrl_tab_cycles_tabs` in `src/ui/shortcuts.test.ts` first: with three tabs open, Ctrl+Tab from the last lands on the first and Ctrl+Shift+Tab from the first lands on the last.
