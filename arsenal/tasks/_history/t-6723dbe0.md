Printed pages and exported PDFs must not strand a heading at the foot of a page, leave a lone line at the top or bottom, or split a code block, table row, image or diagram that fits on one page (D-30, status/ideas.md). `src/themes/print.css` already asks for this, but WebKit ignores `break-after: avoid`. The `.chunk` wrappers (`src/render/enhance/enhance.css`) may also put a heading as the last child of its own box, so nothing keeps it with what follows. Find what the engine honours: one option is a heading that reserves room below itself (`::after` with a block height and a matching negative margin, inside `break-inside: avoid`). Then add `tr { break-inside: avoid }` and check the result with Export on the fixture and the three READMEs. The owner judges the exported PDF in the PR.

## Acceptance gate

```bash
npx playwright test --project=webkit e2e/print.spec.ts -g "keeps headings with their text"
```
